## Overview

- An integrated digital fleet management system that streamlines vehicle bookings, trip 
logging, and inspection workflows for drivers and staff across World Vision Zambia (WVZ). 
- Enhanced accountability and donor-compliant reporting by implementing cost-centre allocation, 
audit-ready logs, and real-time dashboards for fleet utilisation, operational costs, and compliance 
metrics. 
- Lightweight, field-ready architecture that supports low-connectivity environments, 
secure authentication, and optional telematics modules tailored to WVZ’s technical and operational 
constraints. 
- During the software engineering, we used the Spiral Model because it allows iterative development and ongoing risk management for WVZ’s 
complex and evolving requirements. Each cycle includes refining needs, building a prototype, and gathering 
user feedback. 
- The prototype will progress from bookings and trip logs to inspections, compliance, and dashboards, with 
optional telematics added later. 
- This ensured the system stayed practical, user-driven, and aligned with WVZ’s mission.
