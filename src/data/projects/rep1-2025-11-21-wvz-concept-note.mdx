# **Concept Note**

![Competition Poster](/og-images/projects/notes-unza25-wvz-mota50.jpg)  

## **Introduction**  
- Moto50 is a fleet management and smart driver-log system designed to support World Vision 
Zambia’s operational needs. 
- The name combines “**mota**,” a Nyanja term for car, with “**50**,” representing the 
year 1950 when World Vision was founded, symbolising both **movement** and **legacy**.

## **Project Objectives**  
✅ **To demonstrate the understanding of World Vision Zambia’s Fleet Management, role-based workflows**.  
✅ **To develop an integrated digital fleet management system that streamlines vehicle bookings, trip logging, 
and inspection workflows for drivers and staff across World Vision Zambia (WVZ)**.  
✅ To enhance transparency and accountability with measurable impact by designing a lightweight and field-ready 
architecture that supports WVZ’s technical and operational constraints**.
