## Overview
This project is aimed at enhancing radiology workflow associated with cervical cancer report generation at the Cancer Diseases Hospital (CDH) in Zambia. Semi-automating this process of report generation with the help of AI and Machine Learning to reduce the turn-around time and boost operational efficiency for radiologists.
