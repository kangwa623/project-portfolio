## About the Innovation Hub (iHub: https://ihub.co.zm/) 
The [Innovation Hub (iHub)](https://ihub.co.zm/) is a dynamic collaboration between the 
[University of Zambia (UNZA)](https://www.unza.zm/school-of-natural-and-applied-sciences/computing-and-informatics) and 
[Infratel](https://infratel.co.zm/), established to drive innovation, research, and entrepreneurship within 
the technology ecosystem. The Hub serves as a creative space for students, researchers, 
mentors, and industry experts to collaborate, build prototypes, and transform innovative 
ideas into real-world solutions. 

---

## Our Mission & Vision 
Our mission is to empower innovators by providing access to state-of-the-art facilities, 
mentorship, and research support that accelerates technological advancements across 
various sectors. We envision a thriving innovation community that contributes to Zambia’s 
digital transformation and sustainable development goals. 

---

## What We Do 
The iHub focuses on fostering interdisciplinary innovation through collaborative projects 
and hands-on learning. We support research and development initiatives, facilitate startup 
incubation, and offer programs designed to enhance skills in digital technologies. Our work 
spans multiple sectors, including health informatics, sustainable energy, cybersecurity, AI, 
and smart systems. 

---

## Research & Development (R&D) 
The Research and Development arm of the iHub is dedicated to exploring emerging 
technologies and applying them to solve local and global challenges. Our thematic research 
areas include Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Cybersecurity, Smart Systems, Digital 
Health, and Sustainable Innovation. Through partnerships with academia, industry, and 
government, the R&D team drives evidence-based innovation and supports knowledge 
transfer. 

---

## Programs & Cohorts 
Each year, the iHub runs innovation cohorts comprising students, mentors, and supervisors 
who work together to develop impactful projects. These cohorts focus on hands-on problem 
solving and practical implementation of ideas, nurturing the next generation of innovators 
and researchers. 

---

## Partnerships & Collaboration 
The iHub thrives on collaboration with partners from both the public and private sectors. By 
working closely with industry leaders, government institutions, and academic bodies, we 
create pathways for innovation adoption, policy development, and technology 
commercialization. 

Through our initiatives, the iHub continues to inspire innovation, empower the youth, and 
position Zambia as a key player in Africa’s technology-driven future.
