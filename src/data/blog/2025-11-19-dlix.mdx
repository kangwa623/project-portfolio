## About

The IndabaX is a way to experiment with the ways in which we can strengthen our Machine Learning community, 
and to allow more people to contribute to the conversation of ML and AI across our continent.

A Deep Learning IndabaX is a locally-organised Indaba that helps spread knowledge and builds capacity in 
machine learning and artificial intelligence in individual countries across Africa.
