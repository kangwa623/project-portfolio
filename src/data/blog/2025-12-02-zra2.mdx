## About

We fought a good fight at the ZRA Innovation Hackathon, but in the end team UNZA didn't make it to the top 10 finalists. 
The team was invited to visit the developer team at the ZRA Innovation Hub under Innovation Project Management (IPM) Unit, the technological backbone of ZRA.

![Cover Image](/og-images/articles/zra25-ihub-1.jpg)

---

## Reflection

Personally, I was insprired to see the developer team at the ZRA Innovation Hub. It was nice to see the innovative team behind the tax operations 
in Zambia and the manner in which they carry out their work, with elegance and a calm mind, in a fast-paced environment. Their facililty was welcoming and 
was vibrating with a spirit of technological excellance and innovation. It was an honour to visit the Hub as a Student of Computer Science.

A key takeaway from the whole expereince:

- ✅ To never be complacant or leave any room for error in competitve spaces/arenas.
- ✅ To understand the business needs and pain points of the organisation or user you are building for. 
- ✅ Your technical skills can only take you so far, you have to empathy with the user, understand their point of view and build from there.
- ✅ Teamwork is key, you all have to speak the same language in order to make progress.

This experience gave me key insights on how to stay competitve in a tech ecosystem, to always be vigilant and never leave anything to chance.
