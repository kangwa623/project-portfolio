## About

A team from the Zambia Revenue Authority's Artificial Intelligence Unit paid a courtesy call to the University of Zambia's 
Directorate of Innovation, Technology Transfer, and Commercialization (DITTC).

**Mr. Francis Kaping'a - Assistanct Director of Artificial Intelligence** and **Ms. Musonda Ngalande an AI Research Analyst at ZRA** delivered 
a presentation about the 2025 Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) Innovation Hackathon.

![Participants](/og-images/articles/zra25-1.jpg)

The hackathon was an initiative by the Innovation Project Management (IPM) Unit of the ZRA.

--- 

## Reflection

A call for proposals was sent out to the student populace at the University of Zambia.
