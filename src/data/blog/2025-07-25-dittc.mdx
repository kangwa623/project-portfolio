## About

The University of Zambia students participated in an innovation challenge under the theme “Unleash the Witty Innovator in You” on Friday, 25th July,  2025.
The call for the innovation challenge attracted 98 students. 20 were selected after the pre-selection to come to the final pitch and from those 
10 best students were selected. 

The Minister of Technology and Science Hon. Felix C Mutati, MP, was present to encourage the young innovators to continue being creative and 
being inhabitants of the powerful den of innovators. 

He said after seeing the power of the presentations, it reaffirms that the University of Zambia is no longer just focused on 
academic excellence but is transforming itself into an innovation laboratory, it is transforming into creating the foundations necessary to 
enable great minds to be Start-up factories. 

We now have potent tools that we use to create solutions, tools such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Robotics and many other 
emerging technologies that you can use to improve upon your innovation.
Honorable Minister Felix Mutati said the Ministry of Technology and Science will work with the University of Zambia and the building 
blocks of the collaboration is that they have to persuade the Ministry that their innovators have knelt before them and proved to them 
that they shall never fear to fail. The Second building block is to put the idea into the innovation furnace to make sure that what comes out is the best.
Hon Felix Mutati encouraged the innovators to work hard to create a Unicorn. A Unicorn is an innovation or start-up that creates over 
US $1billion which is privately owned and not listed on the share capital.
He also announced that Government has allocated K26 Million for Start-ups.
