## About

 On May 30th, the IEEE University of Zambia Student Branch had the privilege of touring Infratel Corporation's Headquarters, 
 where we engaged with some of the nation's leading experts in data center operations and tower infrastructure leasing.

![NOC](/og-images/articles/infratel25-1.jpg)

The visit offered valuable insights into Zambia’s rapidly evolving ICT backbone, and we were inspired by the expertise and innovation demonstrated by 
the Infratel Corporation team.

The IEEE Student Branch expressed sincere gratitude to Infratel Corporation for opening their doors and fostering this learning opportunity.

## Reflection

My specialisation within my BSc Computer Science is **Computer Systems Engineering**.

A course taught in the Department of Computing and Informatics by Dr. Evans Lampi at the University of Zambia.
