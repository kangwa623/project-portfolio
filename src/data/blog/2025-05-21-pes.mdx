## About

The IEEE University of Zambia Student Branch celebrated this year's IEEE PES Day with a comprehensive presentation on the projects 
being implemented by various stakeholders in Zambia to improve the Electrification rate in the country.
In-line with the theme "Clean Energy, Smarter Grids, Better Lives"
The presentation was delivered by Eng. Elvis Nyirenda, MSc, BEng, FEIZ a seasoned Renewable Energy Engineer working for the 
Zambia Electricity Supply Corporation (ZESCO) Limited.

![Cover Image](/og-images/articles/pes25-1.jpg)

This was an incredible opportunity for the students to appreciate the Energy projects being implemented in Zambia.
