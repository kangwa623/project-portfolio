## About

🚀 **Amazing visit to the Chisamba 100MW Solar Plant!** ☀

IEEE University of Zambia Student Branch went on an enlightening educational trip to the Chisamba 100MW Solar plant.

The members got a firsthand look at the future of renewable energy, diving deep into:
- 🔋 **The scale of operations:** Seeing acres of photovoltaic panels up close truly put the massive potential of solar energy into perspective.
- ⚡ **The technology behind the magic:** We learned about inverters, storage systems, and the smart grid technology that makes it all possible.
- 📊 **The real-world challenges & solutions:** From site maintenance and efficiency optimization to the economics of large-scale solar farms, 
the discussion was incredibly insightful.

Special thanks to our fantastic host **Eng. Leonard Nyirenda** and the team for their generosity, time, and for answering our endless questions.
Their work is genuinely powering a brighter, more sustainable future.

![Engineer at work](/og-images/articles/ieee25-chisamba-1.jpg)

This trip reinforced the crucial role future engineers, play in innovating and advancing the renewable energy sector. 
The theoretical concepts from our classrooms came to life that today!
