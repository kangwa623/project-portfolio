## About

A special and heartfelt thank you to our distinguished speaker, **Prof Vikass Monebhurrun** (Vice President, IEEE Antennas & Propagation Society, 
University Paris-Saclay), for his incredibly insightful presentation on "**Human Exposure to Electromagnetic Fields - From 1G to 5G**." 
His expertise illuminated a complex and highly relevant topic for all the attendees.
