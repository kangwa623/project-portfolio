## About

Our project Mota50, a Fleet Management System meant to boost the operational efficiencies of World Vision Zambia (WVZ) 
was selected for presentation at the WVZ Head Office in front of a team of pannelists/judges. 

We delivered a 90-minute demonstated our system and technical brief to the WVZ Global Technology & Digital Solutions.

I worked on this project with my colleague, **Donald Mazole** under the supervision of **Dr. Mayumbo Nyirenda**. We are all under the 
**Department of Computing and Informatics** at the University of Zambia.

![Cover Image](/og-images/articles/wvz26-1.jpg)

We look forward to the final results and the outcome of this Innovation Challenge. May the best solution win 🏆 
for the greater good of solving real world development challenges experienced by the World Vision Zambia.
I am eager to see the other submisisons by Universities from across the Nation and the impact that this initiative will bring.
